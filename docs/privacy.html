HOL — Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 11, 2026

HOL ("the game") is a mobile game developed and published by Orbyteon ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data is processed when you play the game and why.

Data we collect directly

The game itself collects no personal data and requires no account. The display name you can optionally enter and your music preference are stored only on your device and never leave it. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Data processed by our advertising partner

The game shows advertisements served through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource), an ad-mediation service by Unity Technologies. To serve and measure ads, this service may process your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate (coarse) location derived from IP, device information (model, operating system, language), and ad-interaction data (which ads were shown and tapped). We do not receive this data ourselves in identifiable form; it is processed by Unity and its mediated ad networks under their own policies. For details, see Unity's Privacy Policy and the ironSource Mobile Privacy Policy.

Your choices and consent

On first launch the game asks whether you allow personalized ads. If you decline, we instruct the ad service to serve non-personalized ads only. You can change your mind by reinstalling the game (which resets the choice) — a settings option to change it in-app is planned. You can also reset or delete your device's advertising identifier at any time in your device's privacy settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your region), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. The game contains no chat, no social features, and no real online opponents.

Data retention and security

We store no player data on any server of our own. Data processed by the advertising partner is retained according to Unity's published policies linked above.

Changes to this policy

If the game's data practices change, we will update this page and the effective date above.
